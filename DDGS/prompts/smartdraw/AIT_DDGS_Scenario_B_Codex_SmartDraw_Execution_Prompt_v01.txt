PHASE 2 - B1 PLACEMENT

Create the B1 utility-spine layout using only required objects and these priorities:

2. Dirty/noisy prep:
   - Place PULVERISETTE 19, CT 293, PT 100/DR 100, AS 200, sieve storage and the two dust-collection placeholders toward the northwest/west side.
   - Preserve the one-way sequence: primary grinding -> fine grinding -> division -> sieve verification -> analytical routing.
   - Keep prep vibration and dust away from balances, LECO, Sykam and clean-general instruments.

3. Thermal/gravimetric:
   - Keep OGS100, F6010, both heat stands, both desiccators, the desiccator bench and BAL-01 as a coherent movable cluster.
   - Prefer the north/Drying-Room interface, but do not permanently move equipment into the existing Drying Room unless the drawing and project records clearly support it.
   - Keep BAL-01 on its own dedicated table, near but not on the desiccator bench, and away from heat/drafts/vibration.

4. Clean general analysis:
   - Place the 96 x 36 clean bench with the Evolution One and AUTOPOL blocks.
   - Place the 60 x 30 clean bench with ColorFlex and Copley density tester.
   - Place BAL-02 on its own 36 x 30 table outside solvent, sink and grinding zones.

5. Clean instruments:
   - Place LECO and Sykam on their separate 96 x 36 benches in the cleanest practical area.
   - Keep their standalone-computer blocks on their respective benches rather than creating separate full-size office desks.
   - Add the listed gas, exhaust, UPS and power/data planning symbols without designing final routes.

6. Computers:
   - Use the three 30 x 24 laptop dock/monitor objects as reserved sections of existing benches, not as three additional freestanding tables.
   - Keep prep, wet/thermal and clean-zone stations convenient to their workflows.

7. Windows and services:
   - The curved southwest and long south walls are window walls.
   - Benches may be near them, but do not assume wall power, data, water, drain or gas.
   - Use the five separate ceiling power/data drop candidates where overhead service would make the layout workable.
   - Avoid tall storage that materially blocks the window walls.

8. Circulation:
   - Use the scheduled 48-inch primary, 42-inch active-work and 36-inch provisional-service templates.

   - Do not reduce them merely to fit optional furniture.

9. Existing instruments and sample intake:
