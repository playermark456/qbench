# Phase 4A.5 Key/Value association

Date: 2026-07-21

`environment_profile = sandbox_runtime_only`

`SBX_ONLY_TERPENES_RUNTIME_KV_V2` was associated additively with `SBX_ONLY_TERPENES_PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE_TEST_WS_V3`. The association persisted after reopen. The pre-existing association with V2 was independently reopened and remained intact.

The runtime Test resolved the expected store binding without exposing it in tracked evidence. `Specifications!U3` resolved to `Terpenes`, `Specifications!U4` resolved to `Cannabis Concentrates`, and `Specifications!U5` resolved to `ug/g`.

The association and dimensions resolved, but the required numeric lookup gate did not: Alpha-Pinene LOQ and MU remained blank after the permitted list-based Test reopen. No worksheet value or formula was hardcoded as a workaround.
